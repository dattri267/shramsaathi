System-Managed Demand Pricing

Demand pricing is 100% automated internally. When an end-user selects a task and enters their location, the system automatically computes and displays estimated dynamic prices directly on the user's screen using live weather, local events, and AI demand forecasting. Admins do not manually calculate or override pricing.

How Customer Pricing Works

  1. User Input: Customer enters their task (e.g. Electrician) and location/address.
  2. Context Resolution: System automatically fetches live local weather (Open-Meteo) and calendar events/holidays for that location.
  3. AI Demand Forecast: The internal AI Engine calculates demand pressure and suggests an estimated price.
  4. Guardrail Enforcement: Backend enforces 82%–118% safety boundaries.
  5. Instant Estimate: Estimated price is directly rendered on the customer screen.
